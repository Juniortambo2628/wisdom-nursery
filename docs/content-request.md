## 4. Suggested wording: reply "approved" or correct

**How we grow** (home page, four steps)

1. **Seed**: *Seed and cuttings from healthy parent plants, sown in clean trays and nursery beds.*
2. **Germination**: *Seedlings are watered and shaded while they sprout and form strong first roots.*
3. **Hardening**: *Young plants are moved into the open so they are used to full sun before they leave us.*
4. **Dispatch**: *Plants are checked, packed and collected or delivered, ready to plant.*
